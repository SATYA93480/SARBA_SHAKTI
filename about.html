About Us

Sarba Shakti Educational & Charitable Trust

Sarba Shakti Educational & Charitable Trust is a non-profit organization dedicated to social development and community welfare in Odisha. The trust works to improve lives through initiatives focused on education, environmental awareness, social service, and community development.

Our Vision: To build a strong, educated, and socially responsible society where every individual has the opportunity to grow, contribute, and live with dignity.
Our Mission: Promote education and awareness among youth and communities; encourage environmental protection and cleanliness initiatives; support community welfare and social development; inspire people to participate in social responsibility and volunteer work.
Our Objectives: Organize educational and awareness programs; promote environmental sustainability and cleanliness campaigns; encourage youth participation in social activities; support initiatives that improve community well-being.
Our Commitment: We are committed to creating a positive impact by working together for education, environmental protection, and social progress.
